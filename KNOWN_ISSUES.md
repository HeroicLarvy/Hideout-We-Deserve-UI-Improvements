# Known Issues

- **Queue shopping list buys one item at a time.** When several queued crafts need more of the same item than you own (for example three Gas mask air filter crafts with one GP-7 gas mask in the stash), the shopping list sent to the flea market is correct (it requests the full missing amount), but the flea screen only shows a single offer and buys one item per purchase. Repeat the purchase until the amount is covered. The cause is not yet confirmed.
